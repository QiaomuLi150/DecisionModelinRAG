# GaRAGe endpoint contract

The proposed endpoint is a supplied-pool `question + 15 canonical passages -> 5 unique passage IDs` task. It is not natural retrieval, QASPER CSSR-U, or a joint-set optimization claim.

Candidate identity is positional `sample_id:cite_i`; original order and passage text hashes must be preserved. The later quality-only stage may calculate `support_any@5`, graded grounding precision@5 where the approved label mapping supports it, and explicit UNKNOWN/empty/outdated missingness. No arbitrary grade weights, abstention, candidate padding, or gold-aware replacement is permitted.

This contract is prepared but not activated because the GaRAGe exploratory population is not yet owner-approved.
