# E6-2 R5 Limitations and Threats to Validity

1. GaRAGe source-document identities and independent reservation attestation are unavailable; observed-content overlap is only a diagnostic.
2. One blank `evidence_relevant` entry remains unresolved. It is flagged and not imputed to NO.
3. The endpoint evaluates annotated passage relevance, not exhaustive corpus relevance, grounded answers or factual correctness.
4. R4D latency/resource values are descriptive native-selector measurements across the R4D topology, not a controlled same-device serving study, sustainable QPS or dollar cost.
5. Forward counts mix registered hook observations, deterministic contract-derived counts and explicit NOT_MEASURED values.
6. Secondary comparisons are exploratory and Holm-adjusted across the prespecified comparison family; no noninferiority margin or post-hoc model selection was used.
7. GaRAGe and SciFact nDCG values are not pooled.
8. No E6-3 serving benchmark, reader generation, Jev, paid API, training or holdout evaluation was performed.
